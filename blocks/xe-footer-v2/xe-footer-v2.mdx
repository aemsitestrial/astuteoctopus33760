# XE Footer V2

The **XE Footer V2** is the Xcel footer rendered with the `@ignite/web` footer
composition, following its reference markup:

```html
<xe-footer columns="5">
  <xe-logo slot="logo" variant="inverse" size="md" href="/" label="Xcel Energy Home"></xe-logo>
  <span slot="copyright">© 2026 Xcel Energy Inc. All rights reserved.</span>
  <xe-footer-column heading="Company">
    <xe-hyperlink href="…" variant="variant">Careers</xe-hyperlink>
    …
  </xe-footer-column>
  …
  <xe-icon-button slot="social" size="xl" href="…" aria-label="Facebook (opens in a new window)">
    <xe-icon icon="faSquareFacebook"></xe-icon>
  </xe-icon-button>
  …
  <xe-hyperlink slot="legal" href="…" variant="variant" trailing-icon>Privacy</xe-hyperlink>
  …
  <img slot="banner-image" src="…" alt="">
</xe-footer>
```

The logo is static — Ignite's Xcel Energy logo, linking to the homepage — and the
banner tagline render too. Below 1024px each link column collapses into an
accordion (`<xe-accordion>`). Colors, spacing and typography come from the
Ignite design tokens (`scripts/ignite/bundle/themes/tokens.css`), loaded by
`scripts/components/ignite.js`.

## Authoring model

The block is a **container** (its `xe-footer-v2` filter): its own fields
render as rows first, then one row per child item. The controls below map
one-to-one to the model (`blocks/xe-footer-v2/_xe-footer-v2.json`).
The logo and the copyright aren't authorable: Ignite's static Xcel Energy
logo, linking to the homepage, and
`© YYYY Xcel Energy Inc. All rights reserved.`, with `YYYY` the current year,
set when the page loads.

### Child items: link columns, social and legal links

Authors add **one item per link column**, **one item per social link**, and
**one legal links item** holding the legal links as a list:

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr><th>Item</th><th>Fields</th><th>Renders as</th></tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>XE Footer Link Column</td><td><code>column_heading</code> text + <code>column_links</code> page (aem-content)</td><td>An <code>&lt;xe-footer-column&gt;</code> with that heading, listing the page's published child pages</td></tr>
    <tr><td>XE Footer Social Link</td><td><code>cta_linkText</code> network select + <code>cta_link</code> profile URL</td><td>A social icon button (Facebook, X, Instagram, LinkedIn or YouTube), opening in a new window</td></tr>
    <tr><td>XE Footer Legal Links</td><td><code>links</code> rich text (a bulleted list of links)</td><td>Legal links, each with a trailing icon</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

A social item's two fields collapse into a single link; a link column's
heading and page are grouped into one cell; the legal links are one
rich-text list. The items deliberately
use plain fields rather than a multi-field (`"component": "container"` with
`"multi": true`): multi-fields are an **early-access** feature on aem.live and
render empty unless Adobe enables them for the program — which is why the
legal links are a rich-text list.

A link column lists the pages **directly under its page** that are in the
site's query index (`/query-index.json`, published pages only), sorted by
title; pages marked `noindex` are left out, and a page without a title is
labelled from its URL. A page with no published child pages shows a single
link to itself. Link column items authored while the field was a rich-text
list keep rendering their links as authored.

Each item is decorated by its own block — `blocks/xe-footer-column-links`,
`blocks/xe-footer-social-links` and `blocks/xe-footer-legal-links` each
export `decorateItems(items)` (applying the limits below) and `decorate(item)`,
returning the elements the footer slots in. The social and
legal blocks also export `decorateDefaults()`, the live Xcel Energy footer's
links used when no item is authored.

The footer shows **at most 5 link columns** (`MAX_COLUMN_LINKS` in
`blocks/xe-footer-column-links`), **5 social links** (`MAX_SOCIAL_LINKS` in
`blocks/xe-footer-social-links`) and **1 legal links item**
(`MAX_LEGAL_LINKS` in `blocks/xe-footer-legal-links`; it holds all the legal
links). Universal Editor events fire after a change is saved and can't be
cancelled, so the limits are enforced two other ways:

- **The (+) menu:** once a kind of item is at its limit, the decorator
  switches the block's `data-aue-filter` from `xe-footer-v2` to the filter
  without it, so the editor stops offering that item. There's one filter per
  combination, named after the full kinds in order:
  `xe-footer-v2-columns-full`, `xe-footer-v2-social-full`,
  `xe-footer-v2-legal-full`, `xe-footer-v2-columns-social-full` … up to
  `xe-footer-v2-columns-social-legal-full` (nothing more to add). After any
  add or remove the block is re-rendered with its original filter and the
  counts are checked again.
- **Rendering, as a safety net** (for example a duplicated item): each extra
  item shows an "Only 5 link columns are shown" / "Only 5 social links are
  shown" / "Only one legal links item is shown" notice in the editor (still
  selectable, so it can be deleted); published pages leave it out.

In the Universal Editor each item row carries its model (`data-aue-model`), so
it lands in the right place regardless of its link, and its instrumentation
moves onto the button or link rendered from it, keeping every item in the
editor's content tree. An empty item shows an "Add a link column" / "Add a
social link" / "Add legal links" placeholder (editor only) so it can be
selected and filled in. On published pages rows carry no model, so a heading
with a list of links is a link column, a link to a social network is a social
item and any other list of links is the legal links item; an item without
links yet renders nothing.

### Footers authored with earlier models

Rows are recognized by what they hold, not by position, so earlier footers keep
rendering: footers created while the block was **key-value** (`name | value`
rows, including their five link-column slots and rich-text social / legal
 fields), and the original **positional** layout (rich-text social, legal and
footer links).
